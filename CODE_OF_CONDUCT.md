# Code of conduct

Estate is a small open-source project, and we want it to stay a place people enjoy contributing to.

## The rules, briefly

- **Be respectful.** Disagreement is fine; rudeness, condescension and personal attacks are not.
- **Assume good faith.** Most miscommunication isn't malicious. Ask before escalating.
- **Stay on topic.** Issues, PRs and discussions are about Estate. Take other conversations elsewhere.
- **No harassment.** Targeted insults, slurs, sustained disruption, sexualized comments, doxxing or threats are not tolerated, anywhere, against anyone.
- **No spam.** That includes promotional links, irrelevant cross-posting, and AI-generated text that doesn't engage with the conversation.

This applies to every Estate space: issues, PRs, discussions, commits, and any community space the project creates later.

Estate is independent of Terax. Please don't carry Estate disputes into Terax's spaces, or the other way round.

## Reporting

If you see or experience a violation, report it privately:

- Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or PR, or
- Contact the maintainer, [@ygreat](https://github.com/ygreat), through GitHub.

Include links and context. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, at their discretion:

1. Edit or delete the offending content
2. Issue a private warning
3. Lock the thread
4. Block the account from the project

We use the lightest action that resolves the situation. Severe or repeated violations skip steps.

## Scope

The maintainer acts in this project's spaces. We don't police behavior elsewhere, but we may consider patterns of behavior elsewhere when deciding on enforcement here.
